Rates used

One way: the flat price covers the first miles, then the per-mile rate. Round trip: the same rule on the total miles there and back, at the round trip rate. Pick-ups in the early window (3:00 to 5:30 AM by default) add the early-morning extra to every mile, one way or round trip. There is no return fee. At the airport you add the meet and greet, the airport fee and the parking you actually paid; none of them are discounted. Driving time (used only for the waiting and long day warnings, never for the price) is estimated from the two speeds: the first city miles (set above) at city speed, the rest at highway speed.
